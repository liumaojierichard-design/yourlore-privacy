2. Summary of what we do and do not do

2.1 What we do not do

We want to be unambiguous about the negative claims, because they are the substantive part of this policy:

  • We do not operate a server, and we do not collect your personal data on any server.
  • We do not require, request, or store an account, a phone number, an email address, or any identifier that could identify you across apps or devices.
  • We do not obtain your WeChat user identifier (OpenID / UnionID). The App contains no code that requests a WeChat login session.
  • We do not embed advertising, analytics, attribution, crash-reporting, or cross-app tracking SDKs. The App contains no third-party SDKs at all.
  • We do not read your clipboard contents. We write to it, and only when you tap "Copy share text".
  • We do not read photos from your library other than the single image you personally select through the system photo picker.
  • We do not perform background or continuous location tracking, and we do not build a history of your movements.
  • We do not use device fingerprinting, advertising identifiers (such as IDFA or GAID), or cross-device tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties. We have no third parties to disclose it to.
  • We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

2.2 What we do

The App provides a local-first way to record urban legends you have visited, collect stamps, and share illustrated cards. To do this it stores certain data on your device only, and it requests a small number of device permissions at the moment you use the corresponding feature.

5. Storage, security, and retention

5.1 On-device storage

Data is written to storage allocated to the App by your operating system — the App's private sandbox on iOS and Android, or the local storage area assigned to the Mini Program on WeChat.

  • Uninstalling the App, deleting the Mini Program, or using the App's "Clear local archive" function removes this data. We cannot restore it, and we cannot read it.
  • We hold no backup, no snapshot, and no server-side copy of any of it. There is nothing for us to delete on request, because nothing reached us.

5.2 Security

Because no personal data is transmitted to us, the principal risk to your data is a compromise of your own device. We therefore rely on the protections your operating system already provides: application sandboxing, per-app storage isolation, and permission gating. We do not implement additional encryption for local data, because local data never traverses a network where encryption would matter.

If your device is compromised, the App's data on it is exposed to the same degree as any other app's data on that device.

5.3 Retention

There is no server-side retention period to state, because there is no server-side copy. On-device data persists until you delete it, clear local storage, or uninstall.

6. Sharing, disclosure, and transfer to third parties

6.1 Sharing

We do not disclose your personal information to third parties for their own purposes, and we do not make any of it available to advertisers or data brokers. We have no advertising or analytics partners.

6.2 Legal disclosure

We will disclose personal information only where we are legally required to do so by a competent authority, and only to the extent required. As our technical architecture involves no personal data in our possession, in practice we would be unable to comply with such a request for your on-device data, and we will say so rather than pretend otherwise.

6.3 International transfers

No personal data is transferred across borders, because none leaves your device. If a cloud-synchronisation feature is introduced in a future release, we will update this policy before that feature becomes available, and will describe the destination country and the transfer mechanism at that time.

Where the App runs as a WeChat Mini Program, WeChat processes information necessary to deliver the Mini Program service (including the association between your WeChat account and the Mini Program) under WeChat's own privacy policy. We do not receive your WeChat OpenID or UnionID.
